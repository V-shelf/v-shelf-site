Questions

FAQ

When does it launch, and how much will it cost?

It's in testing now. It'll be a small one-time price, no subscription. Join the list and you'll get one email the day it's out.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows PC with Steam. At launch, V-Shelf runs inside Wallpaper Engine or the free Lively Wallpaper app.

Is my Steam account safe?

V-Shelf never asks for your Steam password. It reads the library files already on your PC and only talks to Steam's public store to fetch box art and genres. To show games you own but haven't installed, you can optionally paste a free Steam Web API key, which stays on your computer.

What about Epic, GOG or Xbox games?

Steam comes first. Other launchers are on the list for later.

Does it slow down my PC or games?

It's a light web wallpaper. Wallpaper Engine and Lively both pause wallpapers automatically while you're playing a game in full screen.

Was AI used to make it?

Yes. The room art and the city loop in the window were generated with AI (Google Flow), then keyed, stabilized and assembled by hand. The shelf, the box art loading and the app itself are built by hand.
